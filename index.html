About

About this demo

A sample portal showing how a regional bus depot could present services, timings and contact details to passengers.

What it covers

Timetable, fares, depot location, notices, contact form and a demo sign-in with a sample account page.

What is sample data

All times, rates, distances, notices and phone numbers are placeholders. Check with the depot for real schedules.

Not an official site

This page is a design demo. It is not affiliated with APSRTC or any transport authority.
